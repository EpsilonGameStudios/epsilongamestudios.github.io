Community Guidelines

Last updated: 11/08/2026, 22:30 BST

These guidelines apply to the Epsilon Game Studios Discord Server.

To ensure a safe and positive community, please read and follow the guidelines.

⚠️ Everyone must abide by these guidelines.

These guidelines may be updated in the future if needed without warning.

Section 1:

Behavior


  1. 1.1 - Be kind and respectful

    Treat everyone with kindness and respect. Respect every user's preferred identity, pronouns and nicknames.


  2. 1.2 - No discrimination or hate speech

    Discrimination and hate speech of any kind is not tolerated.


  3. 1.3 - Respect user boundaries

    Respect everyone's boundaries. Do not repeatedly direct message (DM) users without consent.


  4. 1.4 - Keep personal disputes away

    Keep personal disputes and issues away from the server - try to resolve these privately.


  5. 1.5 - No begging

    Begging or asking for any items, subscriptions, gifts or currency is not allowed.


  6. 1.6 - No harassment or toxic behavior

    Harassment or toxic behavior is not tolerated under any circumstances.

Section 2:

Text Channels


  1. 2.1 - Use channels for their intended purpose

    Stay on-topic in the designated channels. Any off-topic conversations must be discussed in the appropriate channels.


  2. 2.2 - Do not post NSFW/age-restricted content

    Absolutely no NSFW (not safe for work) or age-restricted content is permitted in the server.


  3. 2.3 - Avoid sensitive and disruptive topics

    Political and religious discussions, as well as rumors or controversies, are not to be discussed at any time.


  4. 2.6 - No spamming

    Do not spam in the channels of the server. This includes repeatedly mentioning/pinging or spamming server invites.


  5. 2.7 - No advertising

    Advertising external, unrelated content or services is not allowed.


  6. 2.8 - Use spoiler tags where appropriate

    Use spoiler tags when required, such as for game or movie/film spoilers or photosensitive content that may trigger seizures.


  7. 2.9 - Do not impersonate

    Impersonation of server members, staff members through display names, profile pictures, descriptions or custom statuses is prohibited.


  8. 2.10 - Do not share malicious content

    Do not post malicious content, such as suspicious/unsecure URLs, IP loggers, or malware.

Section 3:

Voice Channels


  1. 3.1 - Do not disturb others

    Do not cause a disturbance to others in voice channels. Avoid playing earrape, obnoxious, loud or annoying sounds/noises or abusing the soundboard.


  2. 3.2 - Do not record voice channels without permission

    Do not record voice channels unless you have obtained consent from all participating users in the voice channel.

Section 4:

Safety & Moderation


  1. 4.1 - Abide by Discord's Terms of Service

    You must abide by Discord's Terms of Service (TOS) at all times. Users suspected to be <13 will be reported to Discord.


  2. 4.2 - No alternative accounts

    You are not allowed to use alternative (alt) accounts. Please join using your main account only.


  3. 4.3 - Do not attempt to raid

    Do not attempt to raid the server. This includes through methods such as spamming, mass DMing or sending unauthorised server invites.


  4. 4.4 - Do not abuse pings/mentions

    Do not abuse @everyone or @here mentions. They should only be used for valid purposes, with permission.


  5. 4.5 - Do not attempt to bypass filters

    Do not attempt to bypass the AutoMod moderation system filters and word/content blacklists.


  6. 4.6 - Staff may moderate at discretion

    Staff reserve the right to conduct moderation actions at their own discretion, even if it is not explicitly stated as a rule. This will be done if it is considered valid and acceptable to take action against a violator.


  7. 4.7 - Moderation decisions are final

    All moderation decisions are final. If you believe the decision is incorrect or unjustified, open a support ticket. Do not discuss these decisions in public channels.
